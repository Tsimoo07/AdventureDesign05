https://www.data.go.kr/data/15067528/fileData.do

Rebuild: python scripts/index-bus-patterns.py /path/to/south-korea.osm.pbf
Requires pyosmium. Each tuple is a directed OSM route relation ID, public route
number and direction label. Matching uses normalized stop name and proximity;
ambiguous matches, repeated stops, final and explicitly exit-only stops are
excluded. Unmatched official stops remain visible without inferred services.
